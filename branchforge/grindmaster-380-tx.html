Questions

About the Grindmaster 380 TX

Will it fit through my customer's side gate?

760 mm across the tracks. A standard UK side gate opening is 780–900 mm, so it fits the great majority. Measure the narrowest point — usually the gate post, not the gate itself.

How deep will it grind?

300 mm below grade in a single setup, which is enough to re-turf or re-lay a patio over. For a stump you intend to build on, plan on excavating the root plate as well; a grinder removes the stump, not the root system.

Petrol or diesel — why petrol here?

At this weight, petrol gives the best power-to-weight ratio, and 38 hp on a 412 kg chassis is what keeps it under the gate-width limit. A diesel of the same output would add roughly 70 kg and 100 mm of width.

What does a set of teeth cost to replace?

A full set of twelve reversible carbide teeth is £186 including VAT from our UK stock. Reversing them first typically doubles the life, so most operators buy one set a season.

Can I hire it out to make it pay?

Owners do, but check your insurance first — most standard tool policies exclude hire to third parties. We supply the manual and a handover checklist; the liability is yours.
