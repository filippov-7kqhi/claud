Built for the job

A flail mower sized for a compact tractor

53-inch flail head

Hammer blades swing on pivots and fold back off a stone or stump instead of transmitting the hit into the gearbox the way a fixed rotary blade does.

Category 1 hitch

Mounts straight onto the 3-point hitch of a compact tractor -- no adapter needed on a Cat. 1 tractor.

Sized for 15-35 HP

Matched to the tractors most likely to be running it, so you are not overpowering or underpowering the gearbox.

PTO driven

Runs off the tractor's PTO -- no separate engine, fuel or service schedule.

Mulches rather than throws

Flail mowers cut and mulch in the housing, which is safer around fence lines, driveways and livestock than a discharge-style rotary cutter.

Questions

About the LFS53

What size tractor do I need?

The LFS53 is built for 15-35 HP compact tractors with a Category 1 3-point hitch.

How many blades does it have, and how much does it weigh?

We are confirming the blade count, gearbox ratio and shipping weight with the manufacturer and will publish them here rather than estimate. Email us if you need a figure before ordering.

What PTO speed does it use?

We are confirming the rated PTO speed with the manufacturer before publishing it. Ask us to confirm before you order if this affects your tractor.

Is the driveline shaft included?

Yes -- the PTO driveline shaft ships with the machine, as shown in the photographs.

Will it cut brush as well as grass?

It is built for rough grass, brambles and light brush. For established woody growth or saplings above light-brush size, ask us whether this or a heavier machine is the right fit.
