About us

We sell four machines, and we know all of them properly

BranchForge started because buying a chipper in the UK was needlessly hard. The choice was a main dealer charging showroom prices, or an import listing with a photograph, no weights that meant anything, and no answer when a belt failed.

So we do the opposite. We stock four machines — a diesel chipper, a stump grinder, a tracked dumper and a compact loader — hold them in one warehouse, and keep the wear parts for all four on our own shelves. A short range means we can tell you the real gross weight, the real gate clearance and the real cost of a set of teeth, without checking a catalogue.

We are not a manufacturer and we do not pretend to be. We specify the machines, inspect every one before it ships, and stand behind them for two years. If a third machine ever earns a place here, it will be because it pays for itself as clearly as these four do.
