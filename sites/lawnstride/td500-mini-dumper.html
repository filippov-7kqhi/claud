Questions

About the TD500

Will it really fit through a doorway?

780 mm across the widest point. A standard UK internal door opening is 838 mm and a back door is usually 813 mm, so yes — but measure the frame, not the door, and watch for a threshold strip that eats the height.

How much is 500 kg in barrow loads?

A builder's barrow holds roughly 40 kg of wet spoil before it becomes unmanageable, so one load here is about twelve barrow runs. On a 20 metre path that is the difference between an afternoon and a morning.

Petrol or diesel?

Petrol, deliberately. At this size a diesel adds around 25 kg and roughly £400 to the price for capability you cannot use. Petrol also starts on a recoil after a month standing, which a small diesel often will not.

Can it climb a slope with a full load?

Rated to 25 degrees laden. That is a steep garden, not a bank. Take slopes straight up or straight down and never across — a tracked machine tips sideways with very little warning once the load shifts.

What does a replacement track cost?

£185 a track from our UK stock, dispatched within 48 hours. A spare is not supplied with this machine; most owners buy one after the first season.
