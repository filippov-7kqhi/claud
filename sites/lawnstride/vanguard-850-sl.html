Questions

About the Vanguard 850 SL

Will attachments from other brands fit?

Yes, if they use the universal skid-steer quick-attach plate, which is the common standard in the UK. Check the hydraulic flow requirement too — this machine gives 30 L/min, so anything specified above that will run slowly.

Is 850 kg the lift or the tipping load?

850 kg is the rated operating capacity, which is the figure you should work to. The static tipping load is 1700 kg. Rated capacity is conventionally half the tipping load — be wary of any seller quoting the tipping load as the lift.

Do I need a licence or a card to operate it?

No legal licence to own or use one on private land. For paid work on a commercial site most principal contractors will want a CPCS or NPORS card for compact plant. That is a site-access requirement, not a legal one, and we do not provide certification.

Can it go on a standard trailer?

738 kg operating weight, 890 mm wide. That fits a 2000 kg+ plant trailer comfortably. Ramps and ratchet straps are supplied in the crate. Check your trailer plate and your licence entitlement before loading.

How long do the rubber tracks last?

Typically 800–1200 hours on mixed ground, much less on hardcore and kerbs. A spare track is supplied in the crate and replacements are £245 from our UK stock.
